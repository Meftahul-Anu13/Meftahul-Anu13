## About

I'm a competitive programmer turned AI/ML engineer and researcher, interested in the gap between what models measure (accuracy, benchmark scores) and what they actually do (behavior drift, failure modes, calibration, uncertainty). My full-stack background (Java/Spring Boot, Python/FastAPI/Flask, Next.js/React) lets me take research ideas into shipped products, and my research background keeps me asking whether a shipped product's AI component can be trusted.
